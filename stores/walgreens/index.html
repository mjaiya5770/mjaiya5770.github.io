About Walgreens

Walgreens is a U.S. pharmacy chain selling prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, beauty, personal care, and household essentials in stores and online at walgreens.com. Its official Coupons and Rebates page publishes digital manufacturer coupons, and the myWalgreens program earns W Cash rewards on purchases.
